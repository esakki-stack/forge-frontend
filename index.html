Forge Grievance Charter & Resolution Policy

Welcome to the Forge Complaint Portal. This portal provides an impartial, transparent, and prompt grievance redressal channel for all registered users and citizens.

Complaint SLA & Escalation

Every logged ticket is timestamped and assigned to a designated grievance officer. Initial acknowledgment is processed within 4 hours, and standard resolution updates are delivered within 24 to 48 hours.

Data Integrity & Privacy

All complaint statements and supporting evidence documents are secured with 256-bit encryption. Whistleblower privacy and complainant identity safeguards are strictly enforced.
